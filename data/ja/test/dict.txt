# A tiny Japanese dictionary for the tests and CI, written for this project: a handful of
# everyday words with made-up costs and parts of speech, and made-up connection costs. It is
# not taken from any dictionary. build_dict.py text turns it into a .xkdic as it turns Mozc's.
#
# Lines:
#   @id NAME [KIND]        a part of speech (the first is 1; 0 starts and ends a sentence),
#                          and how its words join a phrase: word (the default), attach,
#                          prefix, suru or sahen (build_dict.py, KINDS)
#   @conn LEFT RIGHT COST  what RIGHT costs after LEFT: names, * for any, BOS and EOS for the
#                          sentence's start and end; a later line overrides an earlier one
#   @literal KIND NAME     the part of speech of text no word reads (noun, number, alphabet,
#                          comma, period, symbol)
#   @scan N                prefixes with more than N readings get stored predictions
#   READING<TAB>NAME<TAB>COST<TAB>SURFACE   a word (lower costs are likelier)
